# 🧵 SilkTrace – AI-Powered Smart Textile Monitoring & Prediction System

An intelligent AI platform that combines Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and interactive analytics to improve productivity, optimize energy consumption, and automate fabric quality inspection in textile manufacturing.

## 📌 Project Overview

SilkTrace is an AI-powered decision support system developed for the textile industry. It integrates Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and interactive dashboards to help industries improve productivity, optimize energy consumption, and automate fabric defect detection.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Many textile industries still rely on manual methods to monitor worker productivity, energy consumption, and fabric quality. These methods are time-consuming, error-prone, and do not provide intelligent insights for decision-making.

---

## 💡 Proposed Solution

SilkTrace combines multiple AI models into a single platform to:

- 👷 Predict Worker Productivity
- ⚡ Predict Energy Consumption
- 🧵 Detect Fabric Defects using Deep Learning
- 📊 Visualize industrial analytics
- 📄 Generate inspection reports

---

## 🤖 AI Modules

### 👷 Worker Productivity Prediction
- Algorithm: Random Forest Regressor
- Dataset: Garments Worker Productivity Dataset

### ⚡ Energy Consumption Prediction
- Algorithm: Random Forest Regressor
- Dataset: Steel Industry Energy Consumption Dataset

### 🧵 Fabric Defect Detection
- Model: MobileNetV2
- Classes:
  - Hole
  - Horizontal
  - Vertical
